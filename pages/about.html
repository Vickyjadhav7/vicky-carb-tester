Our Story

Crafting timeless elegance and modern sophistication since 2020

Who We Are

A Legacy of Craftsmanship

Lumière Jewels was born from a passion for exceptional jewellery and a commitment to making luxury accessible. Founded in 2020, we've grown from a small family business into a trusted name in premium silver, gold-plated, and moissanite jewellery.

Our journey began with a simple belief: everyone deserves to own beautiful jewellery that doesn't compromise on quality. From traditional kadas and chudiyan to contemporary moissanite pieces, every item in our collection tells a story of dedication and artistry.

Our Mission

Elegance Within Reach

We believe that fine jewellery should be an everyday luxury, not a distant dream. Our mission is to bring you meticulously crafted pieces that combine traditional craftsmanship with modern design sensibilities.

Each piece is carefully selected and quality-checked to ensure it meets our high standards. From sourcing the finest 925 sterling silver to partnering with skilled artisans, we leave no stone unturned in our pursuit of excellence.

Quality First

Every piece undergoes rigorous quality checks. We use only certified 925 sterling silver and premium materials.

Customer Love

Our customers are family. We're committed to exceptional service and ensuring your complete satisfaction.

Ethical Practices

We source responsibly and work with artisans who share our values of fair trade and sustainability.
